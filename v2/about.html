    about

    a standing note

    This is a room, not a storefront. I write here to find out what I think, which means most of these essays are arguments I am having with myself and losing politely. Nothing is optimised: there is no newsletter, no counter of readers, no schedule that I keep.

    The subjects are whatever survives a long walk — attention, boredom, habit, the odd book that refuses to leave. If a piece reads as unfinished, that is usually accurate. I would rather publish a thought mid-flight than let it cool into something tidy and false.

    You are welcome to read, disagree, and take whatever is useful.
